Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Policy explains how Afams Limited collects, uses, and protects your personal data on MeetNMingle, in line with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: email address and password (handled securely by our authentication provider).
  • Profile data: display name, date of birth, gender and preferences, county/town, bio, interests, profile photo.
  • Activity data: likes/dislikes, matches, messages, ratings you give and receive, reports you submit.
  • Payment data: for subscriptions, your phone number is shared with our payment processor (e.g. Safaricom M-Pesa or Paystack) to process payment. We do not store your M-Pesa PIN or full card details.

2. How we use it

  • To create and run your profile, and to show you relevant matches.
  • To operate safety features — ratings, fraud reporting, and blocking.
  • To process subscription payments and provide premium features.
  • To investigate reports of fraud, harassment, or other Terms violations.

3. Photos & view-once media

When you send a photo marked "view once", it is permanently deleted from our storage the moment the recipient opens it. We do not retain a copy afterward. We cannot, however, prevent someone from photographing or screen-recording their own screen while viewing it — no app can guarantee that.

4. Who can see your data

Other users see only what you choose to put on your profile, plus your average rating. Messages are visible only to you and your match. Reports you submit are visible only to you and to MeetNMingle's moderators — the person you report cannot see that you reported them.

5. Data storage & security

Data is stored with Supabase (Postgres, EU-West region) with row-level access controls so that, in general, users can only read and write their own data or data explicitly shared with them (like a match's conversation).

6. Your rights

You can edit or delete most of your profile data at any time from the app. To request full account deletion or a copy of your data, contact us via afams.co.ke. We will action deletion requests within a reasonable time, except where we're required to retain data (e.g. an open fraud investigation).

7. Age restriction

MeetNMingle is for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, reach us via the contact details on afams.co.ke.
